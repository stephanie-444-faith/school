# "We're Not Gonna Bow Down"
### A Purim play song: Mordechai and Haman at the palace gate
*(Sung to the tune of "We're Not Gonna Take It")*

Every line is written to match the syllable count and beat of the original line it replaces.
Mordechai starts alone; by the end, the whole cast is on his side.

**Characters:** HAMAN, MORDECHAI, CHORUS (the people of Shushan)

*(Mordechai sits at the palace gate. Everyone else is bowing. Haman storms up to him, red in the face.)*

**HAMAN** *(yelling)*: Just WHAT do you think you're DOING?!

**MORDECHAI** *(jumping up)*: I'm STANDING!

*(The music kicks in. Everyone shakes their graggers!)*

---

**MORDECHAI (Chorus):** *(sing twice)*
I'm not gonna bow down,
No, I'm not gonna bow down,
I'm not gonna bow to Haman, no!

**MORDECHAI (Verse 1):**
I'm sitting by the palace,
And Haman's full of malice,
He wants a bow, but I won't bend!
I stand up straight and tall, you see,
You can't control my knees, 'cause
Hashem's my King until the end!

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: BOW! I said BOW!

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Chorus):** *(sing twice; the crowd stands up and joins Mordechai)*
We're not gonna bow down,
No, we're not gonna bow down,
We're not gonna bow to Haman, no!

---

**MORDECHAI (Verse 2):**
Oh, you're so full of bragging,
Your mouth just keeps on yapping,
I don't want anything at all from you!
Your hat is big and pointy,
Silly and dis-ap-pointy,
We'll eat it when the show is through!

*(Everyone holds up a hamantasch. Haman grabs his hat in horror.)*

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Chorus):** *(sing twice)*
We're not gonna bow down,
No, we're not gonna bow down,
We're not gonna bow to Haman, no!

---

**BRIDGE (Mordechai shouts, everyone answers):**
Oh, oh!
Stand tall! *(Yeah!)* Don't fall! *(Yeah!)*
Not now! *(Yeah!)* Won't bow! *(Yeah!)*

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Fine! I'll throw the pur, I'll pick a day, and I'll go tell the king!

**MORDECHAI** *(spoken)*: Go ahead! Hashem has a plan for everything!

---

**EVERYONE (Final Chorus):** *(sing twice, getting louder)*
We're not gonna bow down,
No, we're not gonna bow down,
We're not gonna bow to Haman, NO!

**HAMAN** *(stomping off)*: GRRRRRR!

*(Everyone shakes their graggers!)*
