# "I Won't Bow to Haman"
### A Purim play song: Mordechai and Haman at the palace gate
*(Sung to the tune of "Eye of the Tiger")*

Every line is written to match the syllable count and beat of the original line it replaces.

**Characters:** HAMAN, MORDECHAI, CHORUS (the people of Shushan)

---

**INTRO (music only)**
*Mordechai stands at the palace gate with his arms crossed. Haman stomps toward him,
one big stomp on each "dun... dun-dun-dun." On the last hit, Haman points at Mordechai.
Mordechai doesn't move. Everyone shakes their graggers!*

---

**MORDECHAI (Verse 1):**
Morning comes, down on the street,
All the crowds, they are bowing,
Here comes Haman, everyone at his feet,
But this Jew will not budge, push or shove!
He wants me down on hands and on knees,
He thinks he's really so fancy,
I'll never bow to a man, if you please,
I bow only to Hashem above!

**MORDECHAI (Chorus):**
No, I won't bow to Haman, 'cause I know that I'm right!
Standing tall, I'm not bending for a minute!
With a heart full of courage, I will stand in the light,
And I'll only bow down to Hashem, not to Haman!

---

**HAMAN (Verse 2):**
Who are you? Get on the floor!
Don't you know I'm important?
Hey there, Mordechai, what're you waiting for?
Bow to me, or you'll wish that you had!

**MORDECHAI (Chorus):**
No, I won't bow to Haman, 'cause I know that I'm right!
Standing tall, I'm not bending for a minute!
With a heart full of courage, I will stand in the light,
And I'll only bow down to Hashem, not to Haman!

---

**MORDECHAI (Verse 3):**
Yell and shout, stamp on the ground,
Turn bright red, I'm not moving,
Throw a tantrum, but I'm not bowing down,
Just a Jew with his head held up high!

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Fine! I'll throw the pur, I'll pick a day, and I'll go tell the king!

**MORDECHAI** *(spoken)*: Go ahead! Hashem has a plan for everything!

---

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Final Chorus):**
No, we won't bow to Haman, 'cause we know that we're right!
Standing tall, we're not bending for a minute!
With our hearts full of courage, we will stand in the light,
And we'll only bow down to Hashem, not to Haman!

**OUTRO (everyone, getting louder each time):**
No, we won't bow to Haman!
No, we won't bow to Haman!
No, we won't bow to Haman!

**HAMAN** *(stomping off)*: GRRRRRR!

*(Everyone shakes their graggers!)*
