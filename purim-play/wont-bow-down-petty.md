# "I Won't Bow Down"
### A Purim play song: Mordechai and Haman at the palace gate
*(Sung to the tune of "I Won't Back Down")*

Every line is written to match the syllable count of the original line it replaces.
Mordechai sings; Haman interrupts with spoken lines between verses.

**Characters:** HAMAN, MORDECHAI, CHORUS (the people of Shushan)

*(Mordechai stands at the palace gate. Haman struts in. Everyone shakes their graggers!)*

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Everybody, BOW! ...You there! I said BOW!

---

**MORDECHAI (Verse 1):**
Well, I won't bow down, no, I won't bow down,
You can call your guards, you can stomp and shout,
But I won't bow down.

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Excuse me?! Do you know who I AM? I'm the king's number two!

**MORDECHAI (Verse 2):**
I'll stay standing tall, I won't bend at all,
Haman's mean and loud, he can't drag me down,
I bow to Hashem,
And I won't bow down.

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Chorus):**
*(I won't bow down)*
Hey, Haman, you can yell and you can shout!
*(I won't bow down)*
Hey, I stand tall and proud,
And I won't bow down.

**HAMAN** *(stomping)*: Bow! Bow! BOW!

**MORDECHAI (Verse 3):**
From Binyamin's clan, I'm a Jewish man,
You can try and try, but you can't push me around,
So I'll stand up proud,
And I won't bow down.

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Fine! I'll throw the pur, I'll pick a day, and I'll go tell the king!

**MORDECHAI** *(spoken)*: Go ahead! Hashem has a plan for everything!

---

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Final Chorus):**
*(We won't bow down)*
Hey, Haman, you can yell and you can shout!
*(We won't bow down)*
Hey, we stand tall and proud,
And we won't bow down.

**EVERYONE:**
No, we won't bow down!

**HAMAN** *(stomping off)*: GRRRRRR!

*(Everyone shakes their graggers!)*
