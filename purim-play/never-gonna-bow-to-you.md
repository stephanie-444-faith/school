# "Never Gonna Bow to You"
### A Purim play song: Mordechai and Haman at the palace gate
*(Sung to the tune of "Never Gonna Give You Up")*

Every line is written to match the syllable count and beat of the original line it replaces.

**Characters:** HAMAN, MORDECHAI, CHORUS (the people of Shushan)

*(Mordechai stands at the palace gate. Haman struts in. Everyone shakes their graggers!)*

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Everybody, BOW! ...You there! Why aren't you bowing?!

*(The music starts. Mordechai smiles and does a cheesy 80s dance, swinging his arms side to side.)*

---

**MORDECHAI (Verse 1):**
I'm a Jew, can't you see?
Hashem's the only King on high,
And bowing down to you is not for me,
Don't you even bother, don't you even try!

**MORDECHAI (Pre-Chorus):**
I just wanna tell you, Mister Haman,
Here is where I take my stand:

**MORDECHAI (Chorus):**
Never gonna bow to you,
Never gonna kneel to you,
Never gonna get down on the ground before you!
Never gonna call you "boss,"
Even if you're super cross,
Only bowing to Hashem, not you, sir!

---

**HAMAN (Verse 2):**
I'm Haman, number two in town,
Your head goes down, your knees go down, so go and do it!
And if you don't, I'll tell the king, you clown!
And Mordechai, you'd better get to it!

**MORDECHAI (Pre-Chorus):**
And if you ask me why I'm standing,
I only bow down to Hashem!

**MORDECHAI + CHORUS (Chorus):**
Never gonna bow to you,
Never gonna kneel to you,
Never gonna get down on the ground before you!
Never gonna call you "boss,"
Even if you're super cross,
Only bowing to Hashem, not you, sir!

---

**CHORUS (Bridge, while Haman stomps around in a rage):**
*(Ooh, bow to you)*
*(Ooh, bow to you)*
Never gonna bow, never gonna bow *(bow to you)*
Never gonna bow, never gonna bow *(bow to you)*

**HAMAN** *(spoken)*: Fine! I'll throw the pur, I'll pick a day, and I'll go tell the king!

**MORDECHAI** *(spoken)*: Go ahead! Hashem has a plan for everything!

---

**EVERYONE (Final Chorus):**
Never gonna bow to you,
Never gonna kneel to you,
Never gonna get down on the ground before you!
Never gonna call you "boss,"
Even if you're super cross,
Only bowing to Hashem, not you, sir!

**HAMAN** *(stomping off)*: GRRRRRR!

*(Everyone shakes their graggers!)*
